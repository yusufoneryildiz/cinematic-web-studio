# 10 · Image prompts for premium-looking sites

Tested with FLUX 1.1 Ultra (fal.ai). Keep the same lighting words across a set so all images feel
like one photo shoot.

| Slot | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Hero interior | `editorial architectural photograph of a double-height living room with floor-to-ceiling windows over <CITY> at blue hour, warm lamps, travertine and walnut, no people, 35mm, soft contrast` |
| Detail | `macro photograph of brushed brass hardware on dark oak joinery, raking warm light, shallow depth of field, luxury interior detail` |
| Lifestyle | `candid photograph of a couple on a glass-railed terrace at dusk above <CITY>, backlit, cinematic, film grain, faces not visible` |
| Portrait (B&W section) | `black and white fashion portrait of a woman in a black evening dress looking over her shoulder, dark background, high contrast, soft key light` |
| Exterior | `dusk photograph of a slim residential tower with warm lit windows, long exposure, deep blue sky, <CITY> skyline behind` |
| Food hero | `overhead dark moody food photograph of <DISH>, steam, rustic ceramic, single warm light from the left, black background` |

Rule of thumb: generate 3 per slot and keep the one whose light matches the others; avoid readable
text and landmarks you don't want the brand associated with.
