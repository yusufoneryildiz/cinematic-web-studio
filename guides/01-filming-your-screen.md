# Filming your screen for Reels (phone only, no editing skills)

Premium website reels are usually not screen recordings — they are **phone videos of a real laptop**.
The room, the reflections and the hand on the mouse make it feel real, and it's what the big web-design
accounts post.

## Setup (5 minutes)
- **Room:** dim, warm light behind or beside the laptop, never in front of the screen (no glare).
- **Screen:** brightness 100%, night-light/True Tone off, browser fullscreen (**F** on both vault pages).
- **Phone:** 1080p or 4K at 30 fps, vertical, exposure locked on the screen (tap and hold), lens wiped.
- **Framing:** the laptop screen fills the middle ~40% of the frame; keep a calm strip of wall above
  (space for your hook text) and some keyboard below (space for the CTA).

## Shot
1. Start recording, wait 1 second.
2. Press **Space** — the vault pages auto-scroll at a constant speed (`?speed=45` = whole page in 45 s),
   so there's no jerky mouse-wheel scrolling. Or scroll by hand slowly with a trackpad.
3. Hold the phone with both hands, elbows on the table. 25-35 seconds is the sweet spot.
4. Film 2-3 takes; keep the steadiest.

## In the edit (any editor, or ffmpeg)
- Hook text at the top in the first 0.3 s, CTA at the bottom from ~1.5 s.
- Replace the room sound with music; normalise to about -14 LUFS.
- Cover image: the single most striking frame (a portrait, a 3D moment), never a mid-scroll blur.
